Before you start

  • The timer starts when you press Start and keeps running even if you close the page.
  • You can move back and forth between questions and change any answer until you submit.
  • Every answer is saved as soon as you choose it. If the Wi-Fi drops, keep going; answers are sent when it returns.
  • When time runs out, your saved answers are submitted automatically.
  • Do not log in on a second device or tab: only the newest login can continue.
